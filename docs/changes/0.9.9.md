## WoWBoxer 0.9.9

- **Advanced mode** (switch at the top: Standard | Advanced; asks first). For experienced multiboxers:
  - Right-click a key → **Targeting…**: choose who it casts on, in order – mouseover, the leader, a squad member or their pet, focus, yourself … with a live preview.
  - On a heal key: **Also heal Bo's pet** in one click.
  - **Edit macro text…** as the last resort (checked live; such a key is not changed on level-up).
  - Every changed key gets a small mark and **Back to WoWBoxer's version**.
- Your own targeting follows your squad by name, so it still works after a regroup (press Update keys).
- **Setup → Reset to default…**: pick what to reset (this character, all squads and builds, WBX macros, game settings), check the summary and type RESET. `/wbx reset all` opens the same window.
- Standard mode is unchanged.
