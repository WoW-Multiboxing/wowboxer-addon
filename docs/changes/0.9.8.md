## WoWBoxer 0.9.8

- **Import a talent build from Wowhead.** Talents → **Import**, paste a link from Wowhead's WoW Forever talent calculator, check the preview (class, points, click order, any warnings), give it a name and **Save as** or **Save and use**.
- Your builds show first in the Guide list ("My build: … (yours)"), with every point and its level, so "Next at level" and "Plan to" work with them. **Rename** and **Delete** sit next to the list.
- **Wowhead link** shows any guide or build as a Wowhead link to copy and open in your browser.
- A character's own build travels with the squad when you send it to your other characters.
- WoWBoxer never spends talent points for you – you still click them in the game's talent window.
