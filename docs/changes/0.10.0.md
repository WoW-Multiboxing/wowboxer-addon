## WoWBoxer 0.10.0

- **Fixed: a keybind could be taken from an action button.** If your **3** (or 1–=) presses a WoWBoxer macro directly, WoWBoxer now says so and offers **Give 3 back** – button 3 gets its keybind again. Remove keybind and Reset to default now put back what a key did before.
- **Words:** what WoWBoxer makes are **macros**; the key on a macro's button is its **keybind**. "My keys" is now **My macros**, "Bind key" is **Set keybind…**, "Update keys" is **Update macros**. Hover a macro to see its keybind.
- **Edit macro…** (Advanced mode) on every macro and every spell in the book – on your leader and on every follower. One Macro editor: Spells · Targeting · Options · Preview.
  - Targeting is drag and drop: drag a target (mouseover, the leader, a squad member or their pet, focus, yourself …) into "This macro tries, in order"; drag to reorder, drag out to remove. Every row says exactly what it targets.
  - In Standard mode the entry shows greyed, so you can find it.
- **New macro:** first choose **Single spell** or **Sequence**. A single spell can cast on an enemy, a friend, yourself or a squad pet.
