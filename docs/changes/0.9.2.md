## WoWBoxer 0.9.2

- All spells now also lists the spells you learn later, under a "Future spells" line, sorted by level.
- Future spells have a lock and "Unlocks at <level>", so they never look like spells you know or can buy at your trainer.
- Drag a future spell onto your bar now: the key does nothing until you learn the spell, then it just works – no update needed.
