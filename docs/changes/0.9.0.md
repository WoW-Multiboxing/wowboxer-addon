## WoWBoxer 0.9.0 – first public beta

For WoW: Forever (launch 4 Nov 2026) and Classic Era 1.15.

- Your keys in a spellbook: spells left, party helpers right. Drag them to your bars or press Place all.
- Level-up: changed keys get an orange dot; Update keys gives the same buttons new spells.
- Sequence keys per spec (Simple / Medium / Advanced) and your own keys in the key editor.
- Squad: characters, roles and leader; send the squad to your other clients, they import it with a click.
- Setup: recommended settings with backup and restore, addon check. Share setups as a string.
- A ? on every page and a 5-step squad guide.

One key press = one action on one character. WoWBoxer never sends input, never casts, targets or follows on its own.
