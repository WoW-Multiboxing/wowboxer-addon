## WoWBoxer 0.15.0

- **TBC Anniversary is supported.** One download for WoW Forever, Classic Era and TBC Anniversary; WoWBoxer loads the data of the game you play.
  - TBC's own talent trees (level 70, 61 points), the Draenei and Blood Elf races (Alliance Shamans, Horde Paladins) and TBC's spells with their trainer levels.
  - Your macros come from the spells in your spellbook, including TBC spells like Steady Shot, Mangle, Earth Shield, Crusader Strike and Avenger's Shield.
  - Sequence macros with TBC spells (Steady Shot, Mangle, Earth Shield …) and a WoWBoxer starter build for every class and role.
  - Wowhead TBC talent links (`/tbc/`) can be imported on the Talents page.
- **Classic Era has its own data now** (before, it used WoW: Forever's): the vanilla talent trees, races and spell names (Curse of Agony, not Bane of Agony), trainer levels, sequence macros with Era's spells and a starter build for every class and role. Wowhead Classic links (`/classic/`) can be imported. Season of Discovery runes are not included.
  - Talent plans you made on Era with WoW: Forever's trees are kept unchanged in your saved data; the Talents page uses the new Era starter build instead.
- **WoW: Forever is unchanged:** every Forever macro is exactly the same as in 0.14.0.
