## WoWBoxer 1.3.0

Extra lines are your choice, Auto greed in dungeons, the same settings on all your characters, and the fixes you
reported in #help.

**New**
- **Extra lines are optional:** WoWBoxer adds a few lines you didn't pick – the wand (`!Shoot`) on caster damage
  macros, `!Auto Shot` for hunters, `/startattack` for melee, `/petattack` for pet classes and `/assist` ("Take the
  leader's target") for followers. Each one now has a switch for all your macros, on the Squad page and in
  *Setup → Extra lines* – on by default, so nothing changes until you turn one off. Off means the line is in none of
  your macros and no sequence step. One macro only: click the small icon on its card in *My macros* (also in the
  right-click menu and the Macro editor's Options). A macro's own choice beats the switch, stays through updates and
  level-ups, and *WoWBoxer's version* resets it. The *Wand* squad macro and *Shoot* in All spells stay separate
  choices. *Copy settings* sends the switches along. (Thanks Talex.)
- **Quest check** (Setup → Automation, on; display only): the leader gets a warning when a follower did not get a
  quest you just accepted ("Anna doesn't have Elune's Tear – share it from your quest log or let Anna pick it up",
  8 s after the accept) and when a follower still has a quest you turned in (12 s after). Followers send their quest
  log ids to the leader when it changes and every 20 s; `/wbx questcheck` shows what the leader sees. Nothing is
  accepted, shared or abandoned by it. (Jakob, 11 Nov 2026 – the same check as in WoWBoxer Leveling, now without it.)
- **Auto greed** (Setup → Automation, off by default): in 5-player dungeons only (not the open world, not raids),
  WoWBoxer rolls Greed on grey and white items, and on green or better items your character can't use (armour or
  weapon type, class) or that are no upgrade for its class and role. It never rolls Need. Bind-on-pickup items and
  anything it isn't sure about stay your roll. One chat line per roll, e.g. "Greed: [item] – can't use". In a fight
  it waits until the fight ends. (Thanks Altsy.)
- **Use the same settings on my other characters:** WoWBoxer's settings are per WoW account, so with one account per
  character each one had its own (Advanced on one, off on the others). After you change a setting, WoWBoxer asks
  whether to send it to your squad; *Setup → Copy settings* and *Send to my other characters* do it too. Each
  character shows *Use them / Ignore*. This also fixes "Advanced mode isn't remembered" on your other characters.
  (Thanks Retik.)
- **Action bars that overlap in a small window:** at login WoWBoxer asks once: *Fit now* sets the UI Scale so every
  bar fits, *Not now* leaves it. Nothing changes without the click. (The WoWBoxer window app's Auto-fit does the same
  for all clients while they are closed.) The bag and menu buttons now count when they lie on Action Bar 1.

- **Auto-equip: turn the questions off.** The question about bind-on-equip and unclear items has a tick *Don't ask
  me again – keep these in the bag*. Tick it and answer, and from then on those items stay in your bag without a
  question (one chat line with *Chat line* on: "Kept [item] in your bag"). Turn it on again with the *Ask on/off*
  button on the Auto-equip card (Setup → Automation). Clearly better items still equip by themselves. Hover an item in
  the question to see its stats, and the item you wear now.

- **Abandon together is a choice now:** *Automatically*, *Ask on every quest* or *Never* (Setup → Automation; new
  default *Ask on every quest*, and *on* from before becomes *Automatically*). With Ask, each follower that has the
  quest gets the question "Rip abandoned Wolves. Abandon it here too?" with *Abandon* / *Keep it*, one at a time and
  never in a fight. Completed quests are never abandoned.

**Fixed**
- **Abandon together works on WoW Forever:** Forever's quest log abandons another way, so the followers never heard
  about it. WoWBoxer now listens to both ways, and sends it once.
- **All spells now shows every spell.** WoW Forever's new spells were missing, e.g. *Divine Grace* for Human
  priests, *Chastise* and *Desperate Prayer* for Dwarves, *Contingency Plan* and *Confounding Flash* for Gnomes,
  *Shank* for rogues and *Totemic Call* for shamans. Era and TBC also had a few missing, such as *Sense Undead*,
  *Detect Magic*, the TBC city teleports and portals, and the named poison and stone ranks. They are all in now,
  ready to drag and to use in your own macros. Any spell in your spellbook that WoWBoxer still doesn't know shows
  too ("From your spellbook").
- **Power Word: Shield (and other friendly spells) in your own macros** aimed at enemies and got a wand line. A
  friendly spell now always casts on friends, and friendly macros never get a wand, pet or `/assist` line. (Thanks
  Kerafyrm.)
- **Edit text** in the Macro editor closed the editor and opened nothing. It opens for every kind of macro now:
  single spell, sequence and priority. (Thanks Retik.)
- **Level-up reminder:** ticking the box no longer closes the reminder, and it now reads *Skip these changes* – a new
  change still shows a new reminder. *Reminders off* stops them all (turn them on again in Setup).
- **Paste a macro:** only one character counter now, and when lines like `/assist` or `/targetenemy` are left out,
  WoWBoxer says so: only `/cast` and `/castsequence` lines are kept – add targeting in the Targeting tab.
- **Action bar names** now match your game's options: *Action Bar 1–5* on clients with Edit Mode, the client's own
  names on Classic.
- **`/wbx team off` then `/wbx team on`** didn't bring the team box back. One switch now, and `/wbx team on` says why
  the box isn't shown yet (for example, your squad isn't in a party together). (Thanks Talex.)
- **A follower's Assist macro pointed at the wrong party member** (party2 instead of the leader) after updates.
  Every window numbers the party differently; WoWBoxer now uses the slot where the leader really is in that window,
  and a squad sent from another character no longer changes it. (Thanks Salamipouch.)
- **Addon messages in fights:** WoW sometimes holds addon messages back (in fights). WoWBoxer now waits and sends
  them when it ends, in order, instead of losing them. The team panel says *paused* meanwhile.
- **Place all with something on the cursor:** WoWBoxer says so in plain words and puts the macros on your bars as
  soon as the cursor is empty.
- The level-up banner no longer gets cut off in small windows.
