## WoWBoxer 1.3.0

The same settings on all your characters, and nothing lost when the game holds addon messages back.

- **Use the same settings on my other characters:** WoWBoxer's settings are per WoW account, so with one account per character each one had its own (Advanced on one, off on the others). After you change a setting, WoWBoxer asks whether to send it to your squad; *Setup → Copy settings* and *Send to my other characters* do it too. Each character shows *Use them / Ignore*.
- **Addon messages in fights:** WoW sometimes holds addon messages back (in fights). WoWBoxer now waits and sends them when it ends, in order, instead of losing them. The team panel says *paused* meanwhile, and the log has one line per pause instead of hundreds.
- **Place all with something on the cursor:** WoWBoxer says so in plain words and puts the macros on your bars as soon as the cursor is empty.
- The level-up banner no longer gets cut off in small windows.
- **Action bars that overlap in a small window:** at login WoWBoxer asks once: *Fit now* sets the UI Scale so every bar fits, *Not now* leaves it. Nothing changes without the click. (The WoWBoxer window app's Auto-fit does the same for all clients while they are closed.) The bag and menu buttons now count when they lie on Action Bar 1.
