## WoWBoxer 0.11.0

- **No input broadcasting: one key press is one action on one character. WoWBoxer now automates the boring parts (invites, quests, dialogs, vendor) with WoW's own addon API.**
- **Setup → Automation…**: a tick for each automation, **All on / All off**, and a **Levelling squad** preset:
  - accept group invites from your squad; the leader can invite the squad;
  - accept the leader's quests, share quests, turn them in (reward: the leader's pick, best vendor value, or ask), abandon together;
  - follow the leader's dialog, quest and flight choice when you have the same NPC window open (never logout, delete or costly flights);
  - accept resurrections and summons from your squad (on by default) and role checks;
  - sell junk, repair (keeping a gold floor) and restock class reagents.
- Everything only reacts to the characters of your squad in your party. Hold **Shift** to skip once. Casting, targeting, moving, following and talking to NPCs always stay your own key press.
