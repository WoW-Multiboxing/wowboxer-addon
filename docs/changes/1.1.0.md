## WoWBoxer 1.1.0

Team help for multiboxers: see what happens on your other windows from the leader.

- **Whispers on your other windows show in the leader's chat** (Setup → Automation → *Forward whispers*, on). Whispers from Game Masters are tagged **<GM>** and play a sound.
- **Follow warning:** when a follower stops following out of combat, the leader gets a raid warning and a sound (*Follow warning*, on). Not after a loading screen, while dead or on a flight.
- **Team panel:** each character's **gold and free bag slots**, **bags full** at 2 free slots or fewer, and **away** when a character is AFK or DND.
- **Shared sell list:** `/wbx sell add [item]` (shift-click the item), `/wbx sell remove [item]`, `/wbx sell`. With *Share sell list* on, every squad window has the same list; each window sells only at a merchant it opens itself.
