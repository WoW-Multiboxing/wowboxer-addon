## WoWBoxer 1.1.0

Team help for multiboxers: see what happens on your other windows from the leader.

- **Whispers on your other windows show in the leader's chat** (Setup → Automation → *Forward whispers*, on). Whispers from Game Masters are tagged **<GM>** and play a sound.
- **Follow warning:** when a follower stops following out of combat, the leader gets a raid warning and a sound (*Follow warning*, on). Not after a loading screen, while dead or on a flight.
- **Team panel:** each character's **gold and free bag slots**, **bags full** at 2 free slots or fewer, and **away** when a character is AFK or DND.
- **Shared sell list:** `/wbx sell add [item]` (shift-click the item), `/wbx sell remove [item]`, `/wbx sell`. With *Share sell list* on, every squad window has the same list; each window sells only at a merchant it opens itself.
- **Warning sound on/off:** a *Sound on / Sound off* button in the Follow warning card, or `/wbx sound off`.

Quests and gear:

- **The leader takes every quest** a quest giver offers when *Accept quests* is on (hold Shift to skip). Followers accept the quests the leader has, also when their quest window is already open.
- **Auto-equip** (new, **off** by default): equips new items that are clearly better for your class and role. Bind-on-equip items and anything WoWBoxer isn't sure about are asked first (*Equip* / *Keep in bag*). Never in combat.
- **What each spell does:** My macros shows the spell's own description under the macro text; the tooltips on My macros and All spells show it too.

Fixes:

- **Forever two-part names** ("Rip And"): *Send to my other characters*, the team panel, invites, follow line and every automation now recognise your characters. Old squads with first names only keep working.
- When sending to your other characters fails, WoWBoxer tells you in chat. `/wbx names` shows how the game names your party.
