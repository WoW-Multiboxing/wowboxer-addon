## WoWBoxer 0.9.5

- **Bind key works as you expect:** pressing a key that belongs to an action button (like 4) puts your key on that button. If the button holds something else, WoWBoxer asks "4 is action button 4 (Shadow Bolt). Put this key there?". Keys used for something else are named in plain words ("Open All Bags"). No more ACTIONBUTTON4.
- **Place spells** on the left page and **Place squad keys** on the right page of My keys – each places just that page's keys.
- **Recommended settings:** "Clear target on click" is now On (the game's normal), plus enemy, friendly and personal nameplates and cast bars where your client has them. Setup only lists settings your client has; Restore puts everything back.
