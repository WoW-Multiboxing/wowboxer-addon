## WoWBoxer 0.14.0

- **"My own macros" is now called Macro editor** – the same page: your own macros and New macro.
- **Macro editor: always Spells · Targeting.** Every macro – single spell, sequence, priority, recommended or your own – opens with both tabs, and switching keeps what you changed. A single spell can now get its own targeting. In Standard mode the Targeting tab is greyed with "Advanced mode" and the target presets stay on Spells.
- **Every target in the list**: Common, Squad, Party (party1–4 and their pets) and Raid (raid1–40, folded), with a scroll bar.
- **Spells tab**: short type names, Paste… / Copy… under the preview, and the macro text no longer breaks in the middle of a word.
- **Dialogs always on top**: "Turn on Advanced mode?", Reset and the other windows open above the Macro editor, one at a time, and Esc closes just that one. Switching back to Standard with unsaved changes asks first.
- **Right-click menus** show the right entries for the mode you are in and close when you click elsewhere, change page, move the window, enter combat or move the mouse away.
