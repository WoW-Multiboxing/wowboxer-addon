## WoWBoxer 1.1.1

Wands for casters, and shared quests are accepted again.

- **Wand fallback on every damage spell** (mage, priest and warlock, healers too): each damage macro ends with *Shoot* on the same target, so a press that cannot cast the spell (out of mana, on cooldown, moving) shoots your wand instead. Heals, buffs and crowd control never shoot.
- **New squad macro: Wand.** Shoots your wand at the leader's target (the leader: your own target) and keeps shooting. Untick it under *Squad macros* if you don't want it.

Fixes:

- **Followers accept the leader's shared quests again.** The leader's quest list only held about 10 quests, so newer quests were refused ("the leader does not have it"). The whole list is sent now, a quick burst of quests is sent as one message, and a follower that doesn't know a quest asks the leader again.
