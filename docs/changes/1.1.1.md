## WoWBoxer 1.1.1

Wands for casters, your action bars stay the way you set them, and shared quests are accepted again.

- **Wand fallback on every damage spell** (mage, priest and warlock, healers too): each damage macro ends with *Shoot* on the same target, so a press that cannot cast the spell (out of mana, on cooldown, moving) shoots your wand instead. Heals, buffs and crowd control never shoot.
- **New squad macro: Wand.** Shoots your wand at the leader's target (the leader: your own target) and keeps shooting. Untick it under *Squad macros* if you don't want it.
- **Your macros stay where you put them.** Moved a WoWBoxer macro to another button? Updates and level-ups keep it there, also after logging out.
- **A macro goes where its spell already is.** Smite on button 5? The Smite macro goes on button 5, not on a preset button.
- **Nothing is replaced without asking.** When a button already holds something (a spell, an item or another macro), WoWBoxer asks *Replace?* first and lists what it would replace. Setup → *Ask to replace buttons* off: it never replaces.
- **Smarter Auto-equip questions:** stats are weighed for your class and role, so a white item next to a worn green, or an item that is worse overall, is no longer asked about. Trinkets are only asked about when they are an upgrade in quality or item level.

Fixes:

- **Forever: WoWBoxer forgot where its macros were after a login** (the game reports a macro button as the spell it casts). Macro buttons are now read by name.
- **Followers accept the leader's shared quests again.** The leader's quest list only held about 10 quests, so newer quests were refused ("the leader does not have it"). The whole list is sent now, a quick burst of quests is sent as one message, and a follower that doesn't know a quest asks the leader again.
