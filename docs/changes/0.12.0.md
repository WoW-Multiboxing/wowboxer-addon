## WoWBoxer 0.12.0

- **Key 3 fixed for good.** If a number key (1–=) presses a WoWBoxer macro directly, WoWBoxer asks at login: **Fix now** gives the key back to its action button. Setting a keybind can no longer take a key away from an action button, and your account-wide or character keybind set is kept.
- **"Clear target on click" now sticks** – WoWBoxer changes it through the game's own Sticky Targeting option. If a setting doesn't stick, Setup says **Didn't stick** and offers **Open Options**.
- **One Macro editor for everything**: New macro, your own macros, WoWBoxer's macros and spells all open the same window (Spells · Targeting · Options · Preview).
- **Automation has its own bookmark**, a step in the first-run guide, and **Accept from**: Squad only, Squad, friends and guild, or Everyone (with a warning).
- **First-run guide** starts again at step 1 from the minimap menu, a right-click on **?**, or `/wbx guide`.
- **Reset** is in the header on every page and now puts your action bars back as they were before WoWBoxer, restores keybinds and settings, and tells you what it could not restore.
