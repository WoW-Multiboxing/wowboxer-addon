## WoWBoxer 1.1.2

Your wand comes first, and *Shoot* is a spell you can put in your own macros.

- **Wand first in every damage macro and sequence** (mage, priest and warlock, healers too): each press starts your wand on the target, then casts the spell. The button still shows the spell, not the wand.
- **Shoot is a spell in All spells:** drag it to a bar to get a macro that shoots your wand at your target (followers: the leader's target).
- **Shoot in your own macros:** pick *Shoot* in the spell list of the macro editor. Tip: a Priority macro with your spell first and *Shoot* last shoots whenever the spell cannot be cast.

Fixes:

- **Wand lines on non-English clients:** *Shoot* is now written in your client's language.
