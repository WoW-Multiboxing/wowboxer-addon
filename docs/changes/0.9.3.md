## WoWBoxer 0.9.3

- **Squad is one simple page.** "Use my party" or "Add by name" to start; every character is one row: click the crown to pick the leader, change the role, remove with [x] (Undo is right there), click a row for name, class, race and sequence keys.
- Characters that are not in your party: type a name (or Name-Realm), pick a class, Add. Race and level fill in when they join your party.
- No confirm button: changes save at once and "Saved" flashes. The one action is **Send to my other characters**.
- Templates are gone, and nothing switches your squad by itself any more. New squad / Delete squad sit at the bottom of the squad list.
- **Squad keys** you choose with a tick: **Invite squad** (one press invites the others by name – they still accept by hand), Follow, Assist, Target leader, Focus and pet keys. On the Squad page and in My keys.
- Reminders only for real changes. Right-click a key → **Keep off my bars**; own keys and extra keys stay off until you place them once. The level-up window has **Don't show again**, and Setup has **Level-up reminders** on/off.
- Readiness, Key map, Plan and Leader presets are under **More**. The squad guide is 3 steps.
