## WoWBoxer 0.9.7

- **Sequence keys for every class.** Warrior, Rogue, Hunter, Mage, Shaman and Druid now get Simple / Medium / Advanced too (tick "Use sequence keys" on My keys). Rogues and Cat Druids get a combo key (builders, then the finisher), Hunters a Shots key with the pet and Auto Shot, Warriors a priority key (Execute, Overpower, Mortal Strike …), healers a heal key and a damage key.
- A **sequence key** casts the next spell each press; a **priority key** casts the first spell that is ready. One press is still one spell.
- Rogue or Cat follower: if your combo points don't add up, press **Assist** first – the tooltip says so.
- Squad: "Plays as" now reads "Follower – leader is party1", and each row says its place in the follow line ("2nd in line · follows Anna").
