## WoWBoxer 0.9.4

- The top line of My keys now tells you about **talent points to spend** (with a button to Talents) and your **next trainer visit** ("All set · trainer at 12: Redemption +1").
- **Talents:** hover a guide in the list to see its type and source; **Plan to** a level highlights your next points and sums them up.
- **All spells:** a search box, "New at <level>" on spells and ranks you just got, and "Rank 2 of 7 · next rank at 20" in the tooltip.
- Texts no longer send you to the website: WoWBoxer is the one tool now.
