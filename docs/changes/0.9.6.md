## WoWBoxer 0.9.6

- **Follow in a line.** Followers now get two keys: **Follow (line)** follows the character ahead of you in the squad (the first follower follows the leader), so the squad walks in a line through doors and narrow paths. **Follow leader** is the old Follow key: everyone follows the leader. Both are ticked by default; untick either under Squad keys.
- Your old Follow key becomes Follow (line) after your next Update keys; the old behaviour is now the **Follow leader** key.
- The Squad page shows the line ("Line: Dorin → Anna → Bo"); change it with **Up / Down** in a character's row.
- When your party is reshuffled, WoWBoxer tells you ("Anna is party2 here – Use party2"); nothing changes until you click.
- The **?** help button is now a quest-style "?" that stays neatly inside the window.
