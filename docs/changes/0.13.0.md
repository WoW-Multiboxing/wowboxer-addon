## WoWBoxer 0.13.0

- **A new look**: the WoWBoxer "war table" – dark navy, gold trim, Cinzel headings, and your pages in a rail on the left. My macros, Squad, Automation and the Macro editor are rebuilt; macros are cards with their state in words (New macro, Changed · update, On bar).
- **Clear on and off**: switches are green with the word ON, grey with OFF. Tick boxes are bigger and gold. Buttons that are not available yet are grey but readable, and every button, box and dropdown stands out from its background.
- **Readable hovers**: hovering the page list, buttons, dropdown items and menus makes them lighter – the text always stays readable.
- **Community and help**: Discord, YouTube and the website at the bottom of the left rail and in the minimap menu (`/wbx discord`, `/wbx youtube`, `/wbx website`, `/wbx help`). A click shows the link to copy – addons cannot open your browser.
- **Report a problem** (`/wbx report`): a ready-to-copy text for #setup-help on Discord with versions, settings and the last log lines. Names stay out unless you tick the box. Nothing is ever sent by WoWBoxer.
