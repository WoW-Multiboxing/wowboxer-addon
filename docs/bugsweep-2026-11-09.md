## What was fixed

**A – settings per WoW account** (`WoWBoxer/Settings.lua`). With one account per character, each character has its own
WoWBoxer settings (Advanced was on in XERRO007 only). Now:
- after a change (Advanced, Automation, Accept from, quest reward, window size, animations, sequence strip, team
  messages / panel, minimap button, reminders, ask to replace) a card asks "You changed WoWBoxer settings. Use the same
  on your other characters?" – **Send** / **Not now** (once the changes settle, one card; only in a party with a squad);
- Setup → **Copy settings** (to my other characters) does the same; **Send to my other characters** sends them along
  with the squad;
- the others get "Rip sends WoWBoxer settings: Advanced mode Advanced, …" – **Use them** / **Ignore**; the tooltip
  lists every change. Only squad members in the party; data only (an allow-list of keys and values; "Everyone" never
  travels; unknown keys dropped); taking settings never asks to send them back;
- the Setup tooltip says settings are per WoW account.

**B – addon message lockdown** (`Compat.sendAddon`, outbox).
- Before sending, `C_ChatInfo.InChatMessagingLockdown()` is asked when the client has it (the diagnose now says
  `chatLockdownApi`); a refused send (11, 9, 3, 8) is not lost: it waits in an outbox.
- Team status keeps only the newest S / R / X; squad offers, settings and automation messages keep every message, in
  order. Sent when it ends (every second, and at once on combat end / encounter end / loading screen end); dropped
  when you leave the party or after 10 min.
- One log line when a lockdown starts, with the state (`combat yes, instance party, encounter …, zoning, lockdown API
  yes (reason)`), one when it ends (seconds, tries refused, messages sent, dropped); the last 20 periods go to
  `diag.lockdowns`. No "Could not send" chat spam; Send squad / Copy settings say once "WoWBoxer waits…".
- The team panel says **paused** instead of "no signal" while it lasts.
- Checked in the tests: team panel, squad offer (all chunks, assembled on the other client), automation messages
  (order kept) and direct sending afterwards.

**C – cursor busy**: Place all / Update macros while the cursor holds a spell or macro: "Your mouse cursor is holding a
macro, so WoWBoxer can't put macros on your bars yet. Drop it on a button or press Escape – the macros go on your bars
right after." The macros are made at once; the bars are placed as soon as the cursor is empty (CURSOR_CHANGED or a
0.5 s check, up to 2 min). No error line.
